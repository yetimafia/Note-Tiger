Game night cheat sheet

  • Join a voice channel, then type /session start title:Your title in a text channel.
  • Players: /character name:RunnerName once, so recaps use runner names.
  • /note text:… adds anything important said in text chat.
  • /session stop at the end. The recap posts in a minute or two and shows up here under Sessions.
  • /ask question:… in Discord, or use the Ask tab here.
