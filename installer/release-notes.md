**New in 2.15.2: Post to website button**
- 🌐 **New button:** each session now has a **Post to website** button next to "Repost to Discord". It puts that session's recap and transcript on the player website right away.
- 🐛 **Fixed:** a session that never got a proper stop (for example, Note Tiger was closed before you stopped the recording) was skipped by the website forever. Now it's posted once its transcript has been quiet for 20 minutes. The "Publish all sessions now" button in Settings includes it too.

Your settings, sessions and rulebooks are kept when you update.
