**New in 2.15.1: smoother games while recording**
- 🎮 **Note Tiger gives your game the right of way:** it now runs at below-normal priority, so your game and Discord get the processor first.
- 🎙️ **Speech-to-text uses fewer cores:** it now uses only some of your processor's cores while recording (4 at most by default) instead of all of them. You can change this in Settings → Speech-to-text → "Processor cores for speech-to-text".
- 🧠 **Quicker graphics-card release:** during a recording, the local AI lets go of your graphics card 20 seconds after answering a /rules question, instead of holding about 8 GB of it for 5 minutes.
- 📝 **One recap rewrite at a time:** pressing "rewrite recap" again while one is already running now waits for it. Last session, two ran at once and doubled the load.

Your settings, sessions and rulebooks are kept when you update.
