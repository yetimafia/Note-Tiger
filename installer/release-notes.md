**New in 2.13.0: connections for other programs**
- 🔌 **Settings → Connections**: let other programs (like the upcoming Dungeon Delve pickup game) use Note Tiger through a key-protected API. It's off until you turn it on.
- They can make **random characters** (full builds with the same rules as `/sheet build`), use the **Party tracker** (HP, slots, conditions, rests), **look things up in your rulebooks**, and send a finished **run for a recap** that Note Tiger writes and posts in #recaps.
- Runs are kept in their own folder, apart from your recorded sessions.

Your settings, sessions and rulebooks are kept when you update.
